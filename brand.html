A name for the game

You liked the Mariana direction, so this second round stays in the trench: five names, each shown the way it would look in the game, with what I could and could not confirm about it. Nothing has been bought, registered or renamed: the game still says “Iceberg” until you choose. The first round (Oddfish and the rest) is summarised at the bottom.

One thing the research turned up that matters whatever name you pick. The closest rival, Krillion, calls itself “the daily dive” and its share card asks “How far down can you go?”. That is very close to this project's “Daily Dive” address and its “How deep can you go?” line, so I suggest a different second line (shown under the recommended name) and keeping “dive” out of the name.

    What was checked, and how far to trust it

    Checked on 24 November 2026. Game searches on Steam and itch.io are not exhaustive. “.com” results come straight from the .com registry and are reliable. For .io, .gg and .game the registries would not answer, so those only say whether the address currently points anywhere: “no record found” is a good sign, not proof it can be bought. Not checked for any name: social-media handles, trademark registers, asking prices, and .play (it does not appear to be open to the public yet). Treat those as open questions before committing.
