Privacy

Last updated 24 November 2026

You can play without an account, and then nothing about you leaves your device except your answers, with no name attached. If you make an account, this page says what is kept, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Playing without an account

  • Your progress, streak and settings are kept in your own browser. Clearing your browser's data removes them.
  • So that the game can show how many players gave each answer, your recognised answers are sent to our database with a random code made up by your browser. That code is not linked to your name, email or device, and nobody can see which answers came from you.
  • Answers the game didn't recognise, and answers you dispute, are sent the same way so they can be reviewed.

If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address so we can sign you in. We don't ask for anything else from your Google account.

Your choices

  • Settings lets you stop people finding you by username, leave the global ranking, and stop friends seeing your icebergs, your answers or your calendar.
  • Blocking a player stops them finding you, viewing your profile or sending you requests.
  • Deleting your account (Settings → Delete my account) removes your username, email, dives, streak and friendships straight away. It can't be undone. Your username is kept aside for 30 days afterwards so nobody else can take it and pretend to be you.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad trackers, no selling or sharing your information with anyone for their own use.
  • No messages between players, no public comments.

Where it is kept

The website is hosted by Vercel. Accounts and game data are stored by Supabase, on servers in the United States. They store it for us and may not use it for anything else. The page also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when it does.

Children

The game is made for teenagers and adults. It is not aimed at children under 13.

Changes and questions

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions, or a request about your information: open an issue on the game's GitHub page, or delete your account from Settings at any time.
